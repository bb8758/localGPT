# Sourcing notes (research pass 1, 2026-10-16)
| Channel | What's listed | Risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alibaba (Yiwu = mass-market beaded) | Listings seen at "$0.45 - $92.40 with 3-10 pcs MOQ" | Durability and authenticity; reviewers report breakage ("two bracelets broke the first time I put them on") | https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/jewelry-accessories/crystal-bracelet-sourcing-guide-alibaba-b2b |
| Global Sources | Example "US$ 0.15 - 0.24 per piece with a 500-piece MOQ" | No independent quality reviews | https://www.globalsources.com/category/crystal-bracelets-for-sale-price_32718/ |
| Faire (US-facing wholesale) | Small packs (e.g. 8mm moss agate x5), higher unit cost | Sparse reviews | https://www.faire.com/product/p_rr85ws5meb |
These are listing prices, not quotes. Real landed cost = unit + shipping + duties/tariffs + defects.

Verification checklist: order samples from 2-3 suppliers per channel; stretch-test elastic; ask in writing whether stones are dyed, glass, or reconstituted; time the delivery; reorder once to check consistency.
