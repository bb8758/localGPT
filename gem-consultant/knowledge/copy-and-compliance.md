# Copy guide
Voice: playful folklore. Template: [Hook] + [traditional belief, framed as lore] + [real facts: stone, size, material, treatment] + [call to action].
Example: "Black obsidian: folklore says it's a bouncer for bad energy. Whether or not the spirits listen, it's a gorgeous polished stone on adjustable cord."
Do: say "traditionally associated with", disclose dyed/glass/lab-made, give dimensions.
Don't: claim cures or health effects, guarantee outcomes, or label imitation stones as natural. (Check current FTC jewelry guidance and each platform's prohibited-items policy before launch.)
