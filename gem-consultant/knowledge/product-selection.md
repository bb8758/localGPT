# Product selection (research pass 2, 2026-10-16)
No public sales data for crystal jewelry exists in what I found. Everything below is a demand PROXY. The real top 5 comes from your own sell-through test.

## Evidence found (SECONDARY / vendor or affiliate, so biased)
- Bracelets: "one of the most reliable product categories when it comes to consistent sales" (https://naturalagate.com/top-10-best-selling-crystal-products-for-bulk-buyers-in-2026/, a bulk supplier with an interest in selling these).
- 2026 trending stones: moss agate, aquamarine, rose quartz, malachite, moonstone, labradorite, peridot, lapis (https://www.gemstonesforsale.com/blogs/industry-news-trends/gemstone-jewelry-trends-this-year). "Multi-stone designs" are called the top trend.
- Carnelian is called a "breakout" stone by ONE wellness blog (https://www.celestiacrystal.com/blogs/blog/how-crystal-jewelry-became-a-2026-wellness-trend). Weak evidence.
- Affiliate roundups mostly feature amethyst, tiger's eye, and chakra stretch bracelets (https://spiritualityread.com/vetted/12-best-crystal-healing-jewelry-in-2026/).
- Etsy keyword tools: "crystal necklace" search down 18% YoY with ~678k listings; "citrine crystal" median price $17, peaks in December (https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/crystal-necklace ; https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/citrine-crystal).
- Price floor warning: Shein's #1 crystal bracelet sells at $3.30 with 1.1k+ sold (https://us.shein.com/pdsearch/Crystal%20Jewellery/). You can't win on price. You win on story, live entertainment, and bundles.

## Scoring rubric (each 1-5; keep the highest totals)
Demand evidence | Ships light/flat | Visual hook on video | Low fake/dye risk (or easy to disclose) | Gift/occasion angle | Margin after fees + postage

## Starting 5 candidates (HYPOTHESES)
1. Amethyst beaded bracelet: shows up everywhere, February birthstone, "not drunk" story.
2. Tiger's eye beaded bracelet: unisex/men's angle, the shimmer sells on video.
3. Moss agate beaded bracelet: on 2026 trend list, distinctive look, small Faire packs exist.
4. Labradorite OR moonstone bracelet: flash effect = best video hook; trend list.
5. Multi-stone (chakra or birthstone/zodiac) bracelet: "multi-stone" trend, personalized gift, bundle anchor.
Wildcards for the test batch: carnelian (single-source hype), rose quartz (classic gift), black obsidian (cheap, protection story).
Avoid at the start: citrine (mostly heat-treated amethyst, needs disclosure), "cherry quartz"/"opalite" (glass), necklaces (search falling, tangling), geodes/towers (shipping).

## How to find the REAL top 5: the sell-through test
1. Buy small sample quantities of all 8 candidates (5 of each), from 2 sources. Set a hard budget cap first.
2. Inspect: stretch-test the elastic, check for dye (rub with acetone on a cotton swab, then check for color transfer; this is a common at-home check but not proof), and weigh each one packed (target < 4 oz).
3. Sell for 3 weeks on ONE platform. Log each item in experiment-log.md.
4. Rank by: units sold per week x profit per unit. Keep the top 5, reorder in bulk, and drop the rest.
