## Ranking signals
### TikTok [PRIMARY: TikTok Newsroom]
Recommendations rank on user interactions (likes, shares, comments, follows), video info (captions, sounds, hashtags), and device/account settings, which get "lower weight." Quote (2020 version): "a strong indicator of interest, such as whether a user finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end, would receive greater weight than a weak indicator." -> https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/
Practical takeaway [UNVERIFIED]: the first second hook and completion rate matter more than hashtags.

### Etsy [SECONDARY]
Two stages: query matching (title, tags, categories, attributes) and then ranking. Etsy describes listing quality as clicks, favorites, and purchases after a shopper sees the item in search; "new shops and listings have a neutral quality score." Customer service record and shipping cost also count. -> https://www.gelato.com/au/blog/etsy-seo-tips ; https://help.erank.com/blog/7-factors-that-affect-rank-on-etsy/

### Whatnot [SECONDARY]
No public ranking doc found. Guides say scheduled shows appear in the category's "upcoming" list and followers get reminders. -> https://www.underpriced.app/blog/grow-whatnot-audience-followers-guide

## TikTok Shop jewelry gating [SECONDARY, confirm in Seller Center]
"Costume Jewelry (which does not require special approval) and Invite-Only Jewelry (which includes precious metals, diamonds, and semi-precious stones)". Gemstone pieces may need approval with authenticity letters and test reports. Costume listings can't use terms like "sterling silver"/"gold". -> https://branvas.com/blogs/news/tiktok-shop-requirements-for-jewelry-sellers-2026-guide
