# Shipping and margin
## Postage [SECONDARY, Pitney Bowes USPS charts]
USPS Ground Advantage retail Zone 1: 1-4 oz and 5-8 oz both $7.90 (effective Jul 12, 2026), up from $7.30 in Jan 2026. https://www.pitneybowes.com/content/dam/pitneybowes/us/en/postal-information/rate-change/2026jul_uspswallchart.pdf
Commercial/label-software rates are lower but not verified here. Nonstandard-shape fee is reported at $4.00-$4.50 (https://support.pirateship.com/en/articles/13216719-2026-usps-rate-changes). Avoid tubes and lumpy packages.
USPS: "if your package weighs 4.2 oz, you'll pay the 8-oz price" (https://www.usps.com/ship/ground-advantage.htm). Keep each item plus packaging under 4 oz.

## Shipping-friendliness ranking [UNVERIFIED heuristic]
1. Beaded/elastic bracelets, rings, studs: padded mailer, under 4 oz.
2. Pendants/necklaces: small box (chains tangle).
3. Tumbles/palm stones: small box.
4. Avoid until proven: geodes, towers, spheres, clusters.

## Worked margin, $15 beaded bracelet (illustrative, fill in your numbers)
Whatnot: fees = 8% x $15 + 2.9% x ($15 + shipping) + $0.30 = about $1.94 + 2.9% of shipping. If the buyer pays shipping [UNVERIFIED for your setup], net is about $13 minus COGS and packaging.
Etsy, seller absorbs postage ("free shipping"): $15 - $0.20 - 6.5% ($0.98) - 3% + $0.25 ($0.70) - ~$7.90 retail postage = about $5.22 BEFORE COGS and packaging.
Conclusion: under about $20, single-item orders where the seller pays postage are near break-even. Bundle orders, raise AOV, or have the buyer pay shipping.
Rule: if profit < ~30% of price after everything, don't scale it. (UNVERIFIED heuristic)
