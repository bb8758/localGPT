# Shipping-friendliness ranking (heuristic, UNVERIFIED; confirm carrier rates)
1. Rings, thin bracelets, stud earrings: padded mailer / small box, light.
2. Pendants/necklaces: small box to avoid tangling and chain damage.
3. Tumbled stones, small palm stones: light but need small box.
4. Avoid until proven: geodes, towers, spheres, large raw clusters (void fill, breakage, weight).

Margin check per item: sale price - item cost - platform fee - payment fee - postage - packaging - expected returns = profit.
Rule: if profit < ~30% of price after all of that, don't scale it. (UNVERIFIED heuristic; adjust with real numbers.)
