## Key research update
"In most cases, the buyer pays shipping" on Whatnot, and sellers can set tiered/bundle shipping (https://geekchamp.com/who-pays-for-shipping-on-whatnot-understanding-the-logistics-of-online-shopping/). This is why $10-20 items can work here when they couldn't on Etsy with free shipping.
Application needs: 18+, government photo ID, bank account (no PayPal/Venmo/Cash App), SSN or EIN; approval time is reported anywhere from instant to weeks (https://racklify.com/encyclopedia/whatnot-seller-application-requirements-and-eligibility/ ; https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/whatnot-seller-application-process-2025-guide).

## Unit-cost rule
Landed cost (unit + shipping + tariffs/duties + defects) <= 25% of the planned sale price.
$15 bracelet -> landed <= $3.75. If a supplier can't hit that, the item is out, however pretty it is.
